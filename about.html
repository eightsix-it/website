Our Story

Started in the Field. Built for the Front Line.

Eight Six IT Engineering was founded by an engineer who worked directly inside healthcare facilities across Puerto Rico — not from a boardroom, but from server rooms, radiology suites, and emergency clinics. We saw firsthand what happens when IT fails in a clinical environment.

That experience shaped everything we do. We don't offer generic IT packages. We engineer solutions specifically for environments where downtime is measured in patient outcomes, not just lost revenue.

Eight Six is a young firm built on more than 15 years of hands-on experience inside clinical and business environments across the island. That discipline now drives what we're most excited about: building and integrating AI that makes our clients faster — without ever putting their data at risk. And we stay small on purpose, so every client knows their engineer by name.
