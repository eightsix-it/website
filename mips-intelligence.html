SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE

Non-cardiac chest CT · illustrative excerpt

“Moderate coronary artery calcification is present. Recommend primary care follow-up for a comprehensive cardiovascular risk assessment.”

Written for this walkthrough. No patient data.

  1. 2

    The finding

    CAC present. Severity: moderate. Cardiovascular risk assessment recommended.

  2. 3

    The measure ACRad 44

    Apply the reporting criteria to an eligible adult chest CT, after checking exclusions and exceptions.

  3. 4

    The result Met

    The excerpt states CAC is present, describes its severity, and recommends the required risk assessment.

What if CAC is absent, undocumented, or the study is excluded?

For an eligible study, explicitly documenting no CAC can meet ACRad 44. Omitting CAC entirely is a documentation gap. Known coronary artery disease, prior bypass or coronary stent, and trauma or intraoperative CTs are exclusions; an assessment limited by image quality or clinical context may qualify for an exception.

This example assumes age 18 or older and no exclusion or exception. It illustrates the qualitative reporting pathway in ACR's 2026 specification (pages 7–8, PDF).
