medical_services Sector 01

Healthcare & Clinics

Private practices, specialty clinics like oncology, and community health centers with multiple locations all operate in one of the most regulated IT environments in any industry. A HIPAA violation, an EMR outage, or a ransomware attack doesn't just cost money — it disrupts patient care. And when patients are in active treatment, a down system isn't an inconvenience; it's a missed treatment window.

Our founder spent years working inside clinical environments — long enough to understand the workflows, the compliance obligations, and the after-hours urgency that comes with keeping a practice running. We don't need a tour.

close

HIPAA audit exposure

Missing risk assessments, unsecured devices, or undocumented access controls that create regulatory liability.

close

Ransomware with no recovery plan

Backups that exist on paper but have never been tested — discovered only when it's too late.

close

Multiple locations, inconsistent IT

One well-secured site and three that became the weak link — with branch clinics that can't reliably reach the EMR.
