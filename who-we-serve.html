radiology Sector 02

Radiology Centers

Radiology is one of the most IT-intensive clinical environments that exists. A single MRI study can exceed 1GB. A full day's volume can overwhelm infrastructure that wasn't designed for it — slowing reads, delaying diagnoses, and frustrating radiologists.

We engineer networks and storage architectures specifically for DICOM imaging workloads — with the throughput, redundancy, and PACS integration your facility demands.

Common Challenges We Solve

close

Slow image load times

Bottlenecked networks that make radiologists wait seconds — or minutes — per series, destroying read throughput.

close

Storage capacity running out

Unplanned storage growth from DICOM archives with no tiering strategy — leading to emergency purchases and scramble migrations.

close

PACS downtime during upgrades

Vendor-driven upgrades that take the PACS offline during clinical hours, halting reads and creating backlogs.
