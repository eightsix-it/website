Assessment our read, and the plan for each problem

One engineering team runs your infrastructure, defends it and builds AI on top of it. Three practices, each mapped to the problems above.

  1. Unmanaged, unmonitored endpointsNobody sees the disk filling or the failing drive until the morning it stops.
    Managed ITRMM on every device; patch windows agreed; unlimited helpdesk direct to an engineer; on-site island-wide; lifecycle and inventory.
  2. Backups that exist on paperConfigured once, never restored, and the first target of any ransomware crew.
    Backup & DRImmutable off-site repository; RTO/RPO per workload; monthly restore test with the result in your report; we execute recovery.
  3. Email-borne compromisePhishing and spoofed-partner wires are how most small organizations actually get hurt.
    CybersecurityPre-delivery filtering; MFA on all accounts; Huntress EDR with 24/7 SOC; security awareness training with simulated phishing.
  4. Flat networkAn infected office PC can reach the imaging modality or the plant controller.
    NetworkVLAN segmentation, firewall rules, SD-WAN between sites with one policy everywhere.
  5. Shadow AIStaff pasting records into consumer chatbots. Well-meaning, and a breach.
    Practical AI · GovernDiscover what is in use; deploy sanctioned Copilot / Claude privately with logging; acceptable-use policy; then build the tool they actually needed.
