Eight Six IT Engineering · Design exploration, round two

Ten ways to make an MSP site people actually play with.

Every page below is a working site with the real copy, built around one signature interactive moment. Drag, click, scroll, throw. Pick the one or two worth evolving into production.

01
canvas · gsap

Pulse

Living network · threat containment sim

A hundred endpoints breathe behind the headline. Every few seconds one turns red and infection spreads along the edges; a containment ring sweeps out from the SOC and heals it. Inject your own. The stack, dramatized honestly.

canvas · gsap scrub

Ignition

Particle morph · pinned cinematic · horizontal panels

Two thousand particles form "86", then morph through IT and AI as you scroll a pinned 400vh hero, then scatter as the page turns from black to daylight. Sectors ride a horizontal track of full-colour posters.

svg · gsap

Saturday 6 a.m.

Draggable time-of-day scene · three storylines

Drag the clock through a weekend morning at a clinic: the sky changes, an alert fires at 06:02, the engineer is paged at 06:03, you arrive at 07:45 and nothing is wrong. Flip to a Tuesday phishing attempt or a 2 a.m. ransomware night.

 04
three.js · gsap

Layers

Draggable 3D defense stack · threat launcher

Five translucent planes in WebGL: email, identity, EDR/SOC, segmentation, immutable backup. Drag to rotate, hover the legend to light a layer, launch a threat and watch which layer stops it. Scroll and the stack pulls apart, one layer per screen.

 05
clip-path · gsap

Split

Full-page before / after divider

Left half: the guy who knows a guy, glitching alerts, a shared password. Right half: calm serif, green checks, everything works. Drag the yellow line across the whole hero; the comparison continues row by row below.

 06
gsap velocity

Kinetic

Velocity marquees · clip-path wipes · colour blocks

Giant Anton marquees run in opposite directions and skew with your scroll speed. "86 the downtime" in the local sense of the word. Sections wipe in as flat yellow, navy, black and red slabs; a rotating text ring circles the phone number.

 07
game loop · gsap

Command

60-second triage game

Be the engineer for a minute. Alerts fire across 32 endpoints; most turn yellow and resolve on their own (that is the stack), a few turn red and need your click (that is us). The end screen tallies who handled what.

 08
gsap scrub · canvas

Descent

Scroll down through the stack · depth gauge

Start at your desk in daylight and sink through endpoints, network, servers, identity and security operations until you reach the vault, in the dark, where a backup was restored this month. A depth gauge tracks you; bubbles rise faster when you scroll faster.

 09
text scramble

Restore

Encrypted page → restore from backup

The site loads encrypted: red scanlines, scrambled headline, a padlock. Press Restore (or wait) and a progress bar walks the real recovery steps while the text decrypts character by character and the page washes to clean white. Replay anytime.

matter.js · gsap

Playground

Physics sandbox · throw problems in the bin

Chips labelled Slow EMR, Phishing email, Untested backup and No MFA tumble into the hero. Grab them, throw them, drop them in the "86" bin and watch the counter climb. Shake, reset, add more. The most fun a clinic administrator will have on an IT website.
