I. A letter, to start

The engineer who answers the phone is the one who built your systems.

Eight Six is a small IT engineering firm in San Juan. We run and secure the systems your business depends on, and we build practical AI on top of them. This page is written the way we work: plainly, and by the person responsible.

I spent the better part of fifteen years inside clinics and hospitals across Puerto Rico, not in a boardroom but in server rooms, radiology suites and emergency departments at odd hours. You learn something in those rooms that no vendor deck teaches: when the EMR goes down, the cost is not measured in dollars first. It is measured in a patient who was mid-treatment and now is not.1Our founder's background is clinical IT: HIPAA-regulated infrastructure, imaging networks, HL7 interfaces (Mirth certified), and ransomware resilience.

That is the discipline Eight Six was founded on in 2024. We stay deliberately small so that every client knows their engineer by name, and so that the person who designed your network is the same person who picks up when it misbehaves. There is no ticket queue between you and us. There is no account manager translating.

The second thing we believe is that well-run infrastructure is what makes AI possible. Clean identity, governed data, hardened endpoints: these are the prerequisites for using Copilot or ChatGPT or a custom tool without leaking something you cannot get back. So we do both, and we take responsibility for both.

— Lino E. Gómez, Founder & Principal Engineer

Chapter IIThe three practices

Everything we sell fits under three headings. Most clients start with the first, add the second within the month, and come to the third once they trust us with the first two.

Managed IT

Your whole environment, watched and maintained by an engineer who already knows it. Unlimited remote support with no tiers. Scheduled and emergency on-site visits anywhere on the island. Every endpoint, server and network device monitored in real time, patched on a schedule that respects your working hours, and inventoried so an audit is a lookup rather than a scramble.2Included: helpdesk, on-site field engineering, proactive monitoring (RMM), patch management, device lifecycle, and a complete asset inventory.

Cybersecurity

Layered, so that when one control is tested three more hold. Huntress-powered endpoint detection and response with a 24/7 security operations center behind it. Email filtering that stops phishing and business-email compromise before delivery. Multi-factor authentication and zero-trust access everywhere. Continuous vulnerability scanning with a patch schedule we actually keep. HIPAA technical safeguards with documentation an auditor can read.

Practical AI

We build tools around the workflow that costs you the most time. We integrate the tools your staff already want, configured securely and connected to your data. And we govern all of it, because the fastest route to a data breach today is a well-meaning employee pasting a patient record into a consumer chatbot.3Build · Integrate · Govern. Private deployments where your data is not used to train public models; role-based access with full audit logging; shadow-AI discovery.

  • Backup

    Immutable, off-site, and restore-tested every month. We do not assume a backup works; we prove it, and we send you the result.

  • Network

    Structured cabling, enterprise Wi-Fi, firewalls, SD-WAN between locations, and segmentation so an infected office PC cannot reach a plant floor or an imaging modality.

  • Cloud

    Microsoft 365 and Azure administration, AWS where it fits, and migrations planned for zero-downtime cutovers.

  • Advisory

    Roadmaps, budgets, vendor management and board-level reporting. Strategic IT leadership without a full-time CIO.

Chapter IIIWho calls us

We do not serve everyone, and that is on purpose. We go deep where data is sensitive, downtime is expensive, and AI must be handled with care. Healthcare is where we cut our teeth; the same discipline now supports a range of businesses across Puerto Rico.

  • Clinics

    Private practices, an oncology clinic, and a multi-site community health center. EMR performance, HIPAA exposure, untested backups, and branch locations that became the weak link.

  • Law offices

    A document-heavy bankruptcy practice among them. Business-email compromise, unmanaged remote work, and a dead workstation an hour before an e-filing deadline.

  • Veterinary

    A veterinary hospital running practice software, digital radiography and lab analyzers with no in-house IT to keep them alive.

  • Manufacturing

    A concrete manufacturer and a corrugated packaging plant. Dust, heat, distance between buildings, and flat networks that let an office PC reach production.

  • Calibration labs

    A calibration and certification laboratory whose entire business is the defensibility of its records.

If your environment handles sensitive data, carries compliance obligations, or simply cannot afford to stop, we should talk. The assessment is free and there is nothing to buy at the end of it.

Chapter IVThings we have built

We say "practical AI" a lot. It is easier to show what we mean than to define it. These are real tools, in real use, built with HIPAA-aware data handling where the data required it.

CLINICAL · SPEECH TO SOAP

An AI medical scribe

Turns a recorded patient interview into a structured SOAP note, so documentation is finished by the end of the visit rather than the end of the day. Encryption, access control and audit trails were designed in from the first line, not bolted on.

FINANCE · FORECASTING

MIPS impact forecasting

Models how each reporting decision moves a practice's MIPS score, and the dollars attached to it, before anything is submitted.

SECURITY · TRIAGE

AI cybersecurity analysis

Triages and enriches security alerts and explains each threat in plain language, so a small team can keep up with the noise a modern stack produces.

We also give away six small security tools on this site: a password checker and generator, an email breach lookup, a phishing URL analyzer, and two short readiness quizzes for HIPAA and for AI. They run in your browser. They are a reasonable way to find out how we think before you call.

Chapter VHow an engagement runs

Four weeks from the first call to fully managed, in most cases. Slower if your environment is complicated; we would rather be careful than quick.

  • Day 1

    A discovery call. Thirty minutes, free, no obligation. We map your environment, name the risks we can see, and confirm scope.

  • Week 1

    A plain-language service agreement: what we will do, how fast we will respond, and what it costs. Flat monthly pricing, no surprise invoices for ordinary support.

  • Weeks 2–3

    Agents deployed, monitoring configured, backups started, security stack switched on.

  • Week 4

    Monitoring live around the clock. You are introduced to your engineer, who you already know. The first monthly report is on the calendar.

You are not locked in. We believe you should stay because the service is exceptional, not because the exit clause is expensive.4Flat monthly pricing, a dedicated engineer, monthly plain-language reporting, and terms that scale when you add a location or a new EMR without renegotiating from scratch.

Chapter VIQuestions we hear every week

Is our data safe if we start using AI?
Yes, and that is the whole point of doing it with us. Private, governed deployments; your data is not used to train public models; access is controlled and logged; and where HIPAA applies we build to it from day one.
How fast do you respond when something breaks?
You reach the engineer directly. Monitoring and alerting run around the clock, and a critical security incident is treated as a drop-everything event, including weekends and holidays.
Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?
No. Flat monthly pricing, no expensive exit clauses.
What is a realistic first AI project?
One painful, repetitive workflow. We pilot it in weeks, prove the time or money saved, and expand from there.
Are you an AI company or an IT company?
Both, deliberately. One engineering team runs your infrastructure, defends it, and builds AI on top of it, instead of three vendors pointing fingers.
Can you support HIPAA-regulated AI use?
Yes. We have built clinical AI tools designed around HIPAA's security expectations. Compliance is ultimately an organizational process; we engineer the technical side in from the start.
