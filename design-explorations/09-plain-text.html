3. Practical AI

Most AI consultants can write a prompt. Fewer can build a real tool, wire it into your systems, and be accountable for the security underneath. We do all three, and we run the infrastructure it lives on.

Build

Custom tools around the workflow costing you the most time: workflow automation, internal copilots grounded in your policies, document assistants with sourced answers, agents with a human in the loop. Shipped and in use: an AI medical scribe (recorded visit to structured SOAP note), MIPS impact forecasting (how each reporting decision moves score and dollars), and AI cybersecurity analysis (alerts triaged and explained in plain language).

Integrate

Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise or Claude, chosen for your stack, budget and compliance needs. Tenant settings, data boundaries and retention configured correctly the first time. Grounded in your Microsoft 365 data. Rolled out with role-based training so adoption actually happens.

Govern

Private deployments where your data is not used to train public models. Encryption in transit and at rest. Role-based access with full audit logging. Discovery of the AI tools your staff are already using on their own, brought under policy before they cause a leak. HIPAA-aware data handling where it applies.

6. Questions

Is our data safe if we start using AI?

Yes. That is the point of doing it with us. Private, governed deployments; your data is not used to train public models; access is controlled and logged; where HIPAA applies we build to it from day one.

How fast do you respond when something breaks?

You reach the engineer directly. Monitoring and alerting run 24/7/365, and a critical security incident is treated as a drop-everything event, weekends and holidays included.

Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?

No. Flat monthly pricing, no expensive exit clauses.

What is a realistic first AI project?

One painful, repetitive workflow. We pilot it in weeks, measure the time or money saved, and expand from there.

Are you an AI company or an IT company?

Both, deliberately. One engineering team runs your infrastructure, defends it, and builds AI on top of it, instead of three vendors pointing fingers.

Can you support HIPAA-regulated AI use?

Yes. We have built clinical AI tools designed around HIPAA's security expectations: encryption, access controls and audit trails from the start. Compliance is ultimately an organizational process; we engineer the technical side in.
