Service Runbook: Managed IT, Cybersecurity and Practical AI

This document describes, in the format we use internally, what Eight Six does for a managed client, how each procedure runs, and who is accountable. It is published so a prospective client can read exactly what they would be buying.

1.0Purpose

1.1 Eight Six IT Engineering is a founder-led firm in San Juan, Puerto Rico, established 2024 on 15+ years of hands-on clinical and business IT engineering. One engineering team runs the client's infrastructure, defends it, and builds AI on top of it.

1.2 The engineer who designs and secures a client's systems is the same engineer who answers when they call. There is no tiered helpdesk and no account manager.

Note
Well-run infrastructure is the prerequisite for safe AI adoption. Clients on the managed IT service (§3) are AI-ready by default: identity, data governance and endpoint posture are already in place.

4.0Procedures

4.1Onboarding a new environment

Procedure 4.1 — Onboarding Typical duration: 4 weeks · click to collapse
  1. Discovery call (Day 1).Client + Engineer Free 30-minute assessment. Map the environment, identify risks, confirm scope.
  2. Proposal and sign-off (Week 1).Engineer Plain-language service agreement with deliverables, response targets and flat monthly price.
  3. Deployment (Weeks 2–3).Engineer Monitoring agents deployed; backups initiated; EDR, email security and MFA activated; inventory built.
  4. Fully managed (Week 4).Engineer Monitoring live 24/7; dedicated engineer introduced; first monthly report scheduled.

4.2Critical security incident

Warning
A confirmed ransomware or data-exfiltration event is a drop-everything priority at any hour. Do not power off affected systems before isolation is confirmed by the engineer; do not pay a ransom demand without consultation.
Procedure 4.2 — Incident response Retainer clients · click to collapse
  1. Detect. EDR behavioral analysis, SOC correlation and email filtering surface the event, typically within minutes.
  2. Contain. Automated endpoint isolation and network segmentation stop lateral movement.
  3. Eradicate and recover. Restore from immutable, air-gapped backups against the workload's RTO/RPO. The engineer executes recovery; the client is not left to follow instructions alone.
  4. Report. Post-incident forensic report and remediation plan; regulatory breach-notification support (HIPAA / OCR) where applicable.

4.3Monthly backup verification

Caution
A backup that has never been restored is not a recovery plan. Attackers target backups first. Verification is not optional and results are reported to the client every month.
Procedure 4.3 — Restore test Monthly · automated · click to collapse
  1. Select representative workloads (EMR database, file server, imaging store, line-of-business application).
  2. Restore to isolated environment from the immutable repository.
  3. Verify integrity and boot; record achieved recovery time against RTO target.
  4. Record results in the client's monthly report (§6.3).

5.0AI services

5.1 Build
Custom tools and automation around the client's highest-cost workflow: workflow automation, internal copilots grounded in policies and SOPs, document/RAG assistants with sourced answers, agentic workflows with a human in the loop. Shipped examples: AI medical scribe (speech to SOAP), MIPS impact forecasting, AI cybersecurity alert analysis.
5.2 Integrate
Selection among Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, Claude or purpose-built; tenant and retention settings configured correctly the first time; grounding in Microsoft 365 / SharePoint / line-of-business data; role-based training so adoption actually happens.
5.3 Govern
Private deployments where client data is not used to train public models; encryption in transit and at rest; role-based access with full audit logging; shadow-AI discovery; acceptable-use policy; HIPAA-aware data handling where required.
Note
A realistic first project is one painful, repetitive workflow, piloted in weeks with the saving measured, then expanded. Not a year-long roadmap.

6.0Commercial terms

6.1 Pricing
Flat monthly fee. No surprise invoices for normal support activity. Quoted after the discovery call.
6.2 Term
No long-term lock-in and no expensive exit clauses. Clients stay because the service is exceptional or they leave.
6.3 Reporting
Monthly plain-language report: system health, incidents resolved, patch status, backup verification result, security posture.
6.4 Scaling
Adding a location, twenty users or a new EMR does not reopen the agreement.
6.5 Assignment
One dedicated engineer per client environment.

7.0Frequently raised questions

Is our data safe if we start using AI?
Yes. Private, governed deployments; data not used to train public models; access controlled and logged; HIPAA built in where it applies (§5.3).
How fast is the response when something breaks?
The client reaches the engineer directly. Monitoring runs 24/7/365; critical incidents follow §4.2 at any hour.
Are there long-term contracts?
No (§6.2).
AI company or IT company?
Both, deliberately. One accountable team instead of three vendors.
HIPAA-regulated AI use?
Supported. Clinical AI tools have been built to HIPAA's security expectations; compliance remains an organizational process the client owns.
